# Tonino: Ear Training

Tonino is ear training made for playing by ear. The piano plays, you answer, and you hear at once whether you got it and what the right answer sounds like. Six worlds take you from "did it go up or down?" to intervals, chords, degrees in the key and chord progressions, in 40 levels of 10 questions with 1 to 3 stars each.

## How it works

- **Worlds open with stars.** World 2 opens with 5 stars, world 3 with 12, world 4 with 18, world 5 with 24 and world 6 with 30. Inside a world, a star on a level opens the next one.
- **Stars:** 7 right answers out of 10 earn one star, 9 earn two, 10 earn three. Fewer than 7: try again, nothing is lost.
- **A miss teaches:** the keyboard shows the right notes, and Tonino plays your answer and then the right one.
- **Settings** (the gear on the map): note names in Do-Re-Mi or C-D-E, progressions as degrees or chord symbols, normal or slow speed, game sounds, vibration.
- **The piano plays with the silent switch on**, like any music app. Check the volume buttons if you hear nothing.

## Free and Pro

- **Free:** worlds 1 and 2 complete and the first level of every other world (17 levels).
- **Tonino Pro** (one payment, shared with your family, no subscription): the other 23 levels, a review that asks more of what you miss, and statistics for every interval, chord, degree and progression.
- **Restore a purchase:** Settings → Restore Purchases.
- **Start over:** Settings → Start over (erases stars and statistics on that device).

## The piano

The sound is the Steinway B grand piano of the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), released into the public domain (CC0-1.0). Thank you, VCSL. Tonino tunes each of its 37 notes (C3 to C6) to A = 440 Hz.
